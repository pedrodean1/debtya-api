Terms of Service

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Welcome to DebtYa. These Terms of Service govern access to and use of the platform, website, and related services offered by DebtYa. By creating an account, accessing, or using DebtYa, you agree to these Terms.

1. Who we are

DebtYa is a software platform that acts as a debt copilot: it helps you organize payoff using information you enter (balances, APR, minimum payments), a strategy you choose (Avalanche or Snowball), and a recommended next payment line. The current product experience is manual-first: you pay your creditors outside DebtYa and confirm with I paid it inside the app.

2. What DebtYa does

DebtYa provides tools to:

  • organize debts you enter manually,
  • compare Avalanche vs Snowball and show recommendations based on your inputs,
  • track progress and payment history when you confirm payments,
  • optionally explain recommendations in plain language (for example through an AI Coach feature) in educational terms only.

DebtYa does not move money, does not execute payments to creditors for you, does not promise automatic payments, is not a bank, and does not lend money. Recommendations and projections are estimates and may vary. You remain responsible for verifying balances, APR, minimum payments, and all payments with your creditors and on your statements.

3. What DebtYa is not

DebtYa is not:

  • a bank or lender,
  • regulated financial, legal, tax, or credit advice (including from any automated coach or assistant),
  • a credit repair agency,
  • a law firm,
  • a debt relief or consolidation service.

4. Account and access

You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential and for all activity on your account. You should provide reasonably accurate information and keep it up to date.

5. Pricing and billing

During the manual-first public beta, core DebtYa tools are offered free ("Free during beta"). If we introduce paid subscriptions in the future, we will show pricing and terms before any charge. Where a paid subscription applies to an account, charges may be processed through a payment processor (for example Stripe) as disclosed at checkout or in your account.

6. Third-party services

DebtYa uses independent service providers for infrastructure, security, email delivery, analytics, and similar operations. The default DebtYa experience is manual-first and does not require you to connect a bank or import accounts. Optional or legacy features may exist for some accounts or environments; where available they are subject to separate consent, provider terms, technical limits, and regional coverage. We do not guarantee every institution, creditor, or flow will be supported.

DebtYa may display, organize, or help you plan payments; it does not execute payments to creditors for you in the manual-first product path.
